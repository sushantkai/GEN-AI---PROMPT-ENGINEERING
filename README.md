🚀 Prompt Engineering Basics

This repository contains my learning and experiments on Prompt Engineering, a key skill for working with Large Language Models (LLMs) like ChatGPT and Gemini.

The goal of this project is to understand how to communicate effectively with AI models to get accurate, relevant, and well-structured responses.

📌 What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt Engineering is the practice of designing clear and structured inputs (prompts) to guide AI models toward better outputs.

👉 In simple words:
Better prompts = Better AI responses

🧠 Key Concepts Covered

LLM (Large Language Model)
AI models trained on large amounts of text data.

Prompt
The instruction or question given to the AI.

Completion
The response generated by the AI.

✨ Why Prompt Engineering Matters

Improves accuracy and relevance of responses

Gives better control over AI behavior

Helps solve complex problems step by step

Saves time and reduces repeated prompts

Essential for GenAI, RAG, and Agent-based systems

🧩 Elements of an Effective Prompt

A good prompt usually includes:

Role – Who the AI should act as

Context – Background information

Task – What the AI needs to do

Constraints – Rules like word limits or tone

Examples – (Optional) Sample input/output

Output Format – How the answer should be structured
